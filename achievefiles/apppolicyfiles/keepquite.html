Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 28, 2026

Thank you for using KeepQuite, a horror survival game developed by Prafuldas M M. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information our application accesses, how it is used, and how we protect your privacy.

Summary
  • We do not create user accounts.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not record or store conversations.
  • We do not collect your contacts, messages, or location.
  • Permissions are requested only for gameplay features.

1. Information We Collect

KeepQuite does not require registration and does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Contacts
  • Messages
  • Location
  • Photos

2. Device Permissions

Permission Purpose
Microphone Used to detect environmental sound levels as part of the gameplay mechanics. Audio is processed on your device only and is never stored or transmitted.
Camera Used only for flashlight access when enabled during gameplay. The application does not capture photographs or videos.
Flashlight Provides immersive lighting effects during gameplay.
Motion Sensors Accelerometer and device sensors detect movement and trigger game events.
Internet Used for application updates, online services, future multiplayer features, bug fixes, and downloading game assets when required.
Storage / Media Access If enabled, storage permissions allow the app to access only files selected by you for optional features. The application does not scan or upload your personal media.

3. Microphone Usage

KeepQuite is built around sound-based gameplay. When microphone permission is granted, the application continuously measures sound intensity to determine in-game events.

No audio recordings are saved.
No conversations are transmitted.
No voice data leaves your device.

4. Camera & Flashlight

Camera permission is requested solely because Android requires it to control the device flashlight on many devices. The application never records photos or videos, and camera images are never processed or stored.

5. Local Storage

The application stores game-related information locally, including:

  • Game settings
  • Audio preferences
  • Graphics settings
  • Game progress
  • Saved preferences

This information remains on your device unless you uninstall the application or clear its data.

6. Analytics & Advertising

At this time, KeepQuite does not display third-party advertisements and does not use analytics services to track users. If analytics or advertising services are added in future releases, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are implemented to protect application data. Although no electronic system is completely secure, we continuously work to improve application security and stability.

9. Children's Privacy

KeepQuite is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the applicable age required by local laws. If you believe personal information has been provided by a child, please contact us and we will promptly investigate the matter.

10. Your Choices

You may revoke any permission at any time by opening:

  • Android Settings
  • Apps
  • KeepQuite
  • Permissions

Please note that disabling certain permissions may affect gameplay.

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect application improvements or legal requirements. Any changes become effective immediately after they are published on this page.
