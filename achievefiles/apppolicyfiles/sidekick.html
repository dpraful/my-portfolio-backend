SideKick

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 28, 2026

SideKick is a sports social networking and turf booking platform developed by Prafuldas M M. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user data when you use our application.

Key Highlights
  • We collect only necessary user data for app functionality.
  • We do not sell user data.
  • Users control their profiles and content.
  • Chat and booking data are stored securely.

1. Information We Collect

SideKick may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information (username, email, password)
  • Profile data (name, profile picture, bio, sports preferences)
  • Content created by users (posts, comments, messages)
  • Booking information (turf reservations, schedules)
  • Device information (for app performance and security)

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Enable sports social features (posts, chat, profiles)
  • Manage turf booking and scheduling system
  • Improve app performance and security
  • Send important notifications (booking updates, messages)

3. User Content

Users are responsible for the content they share. Posts, chats, and profile information are stored to provide app functionality. Users may edit or delete their content at any time where applicable.

4. Data Storage

All data is stored securely in backend servers (FastAPI + MySQL architecture). We use standard security practices such as authentication and access control to protect user data.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data only in the following cases:

  • With service providers for hosting and infrastructure
  • When required by law or legal process
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, or security threats

6. Chat & Communication

SideKick includes messaging features between users. Messages are stored to ensure delivery and chat history. We do not monitor private conversations unless required for safety or legal reasons.

7. Turf Booking System

Booking data such as turf name, time slots, and user details are stored to manage reservations and avoid conflicts.

8. Analytics & Performance

We may use basic analytics tools to improve app performance, such as crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify individual users.

9. Data Security

We implement security measures including:

  • JWT-based authentication
  • Encrypted API communication (HTTPS)
  • Role-based access control
  • Secure database storage

10. Children's Privacy

SideKick is not intended for users under the age required by local laws. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Your Rights

  • Access your profile data
  • Update or delete your account
  • Control privacy settings
  • Request data deletion (where applicable)

12. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as:

  • Cloud hosting providers
  • Notification services
  • Analytics tools

These services follow their own privacy policies.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in the app or on our website.
